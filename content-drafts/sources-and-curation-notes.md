My intent in creating my original file was to provide a shopping list of
sorts to guide my record store browsing.

## Sources

- Best of 2007-2024 lists, as well the special drive lists, were created
  by capturing albums as they were revealed on air, then reconciling
  and/or correcting the entries when they were posted to KEXP.org
- Best of 2001-2006 lists were graciously provided by KEXP staff and
  reconciled with historical playlists

## List Curation Notes

I have made some decisions to help with the organization and navigation
of this workbook. Admittedly, there may be better ways to handle some of
these things, but this is what works for me.

### Artist and Album Names

For official artist and album names, I have used a combination of
MusicBrainz, AllMusic, and Discogs. In some cases, I have made small
adjustments to help with organization.

- **Consistent Naming.** One of the most frequent adjustments I have made
  is the consistent application of "and" or "&" for a given artist. In the
  case of Neil Young & Crazy Horse, this extends to "with". I have also
  tried to be consistent in using "The" when applicable, even if the bands
  themselves haven't been.
- **Stylized Naming.** I've been kind of hit-or-miss with this, but when
  an artist has been consistent in how they refer to themselves over a
  few albums, I've tried to stick with their capitalization style. Some
  examples: *IDLES*, *boygenius*, *tUnE-yArDs*.
- **Manual Adjustments.** In a handful of cases, I have made educated
  guesses on how to treat ambiguous list data. See the bottom of this
  README for a detailed list of these adjustments.

### Manual Adjustments and Notes

- **KEXP Top 903**
  - The Go! Team - *Thunder, Lightning, Strike*. Played twice (494 and
    881). The 881 entry was listed as *The Power Is On*, which is a
    single from *Thunder, Lightning, Strike*.
- **KEXP 21st Century**
  - Radiohead - *OK Computer*. Played twice (34 and 58). The play
    associated with the 58 entry was *Climbing Up the Walls (BBC Radio 1
    Session)*, a track from *OK Computer (Collector's Edition)*.
  - Death Cab For Cutie - *Plans*. Played twice (52 and 418). The play
    associated with the 418 entry was *World Shut Your Mouth*, a B-side
    to *Crooked Teeth* from the *Plans* album. The playlist listed the
    album as *Title and Registration*, a track from the *Transatlanticism*
    album (which is listed at 14).
- **KEXP Top 666**
  - Florence + The Machine - *Moderation*. Listed at 571. This is a
    single.
- **KEXP 50 Years**
  - *Blue Album* and *Weezer* were listed separately, at 62 and 222,
    respectively. I made the assumption that this was Weezer's debut
    album listed twice. I don't believe any of the other self-titled
    Weezer albums would have slotted in before *Pinkerton* at 544.
  - No album was assigned to rank slot 453 due to a KEXP counting error.
- **KEXP All Time**
  - List updated after it was aired due to a KEXP data transfer issue. 72
    albums with Th-Z artists excluded from original list.
- **Best of 2005**
  - The Postal Service - *We Will Become Silhouettes*. Listed at 29. This
    is a 4-track single.
- **Best of 2008**
  - The Decemberists - *Always the Bridesmaid: A Single Series*. Listed
    at 73. This is a single.
- **Best of 2018**
  - Changed *MassEduction* to *MassEducation2* to differentiate St.
    Vincent's two albums with the same name.
