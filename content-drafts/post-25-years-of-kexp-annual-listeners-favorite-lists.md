# 25 years of KEXP Annual Listeners' Favorite lists

In 25 years of KEXP's listener-voted Annual "Listeners' Favorite Albums of
the Year" lists, exactly three artists have ever landed ten or more albums on one:
King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard, Wilco, and The Decemberists. Not top ten,
not top five — three, total, in a quarter-century, out of every artist
who's ever made a list.

That number is what got me looking at the full 25-year span in one place
instead of one list at a time. So I pulled every entry from every KEXP
Annual list, 2001 through 2025, and built the thing below: one wheel, one
spoke per year, every artist who ever made the cut.

Each dot's color and size say the same thing — how many distinct albums
that artist has had on an Annual list across the entire 25 years, not just
this one year. Search for an artist to see their own spokes highlighted
across every year they showed up, or use the dropdown to isolate specific
years.

That three-artist top tier isn't a round number I picked — it's a real
cliff in the data. Going from 9 albums to 10 is where the population
actually falls off; every cutoff near it is a gentle, steady slope by
comparison. And the more common story is the opposite end entirely: 53.3%
of every artist who's ever made an Annual list has exactly *one* album on
it, ever. "One and done" is the normal case here, not the exception —
which says something about what a listener-voted list actually rewards
over 25 years: less a fixed canon of favorites, more a wide, constantly
refreshing door.
